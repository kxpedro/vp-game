# Combat sound sources

The original swing and sword-hit files are CC0 1.0. The flesh subfolder uses OGA-BY 3.0.

- `Sword Swing *.wav`, `Greatsword *.wav` and `Dodge *.wav`: variações selecionadas ou editadas de “Swishes Sound Pack” by artisticdude, downloaded from https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
- `Sword Hit *.ogg`: “20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)” by StarNinjas, downloaded from https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes
- `Revolver Shot 1.wav`, `Revolver Shot 2.wav`, `Revolver Shot Heavy.wav` e `Revolver Reload.wav`: sintetizados para o projeto (ruído + senoides, sem amostras de terceiros) por `tools/generate_revolver_sfx.py`; CC0 1.0.
- `flesh/*.wav`: “Fleshy Fight Sounds” by Will Leamon, downloaded from https://opengameart.org/content/fleshy-fight-sounds and used under OGA-BY 3.0. Attribution: “Additional Sound FX by Will Leamon”.

The original filenames were renamed for their runtime roles. The swishes and sword pack are CC0; the flesh pack requires the attribution retained above.
